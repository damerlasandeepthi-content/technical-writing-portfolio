#How to Create a Gmail Account

##Purpose

This guide explains how to create a new Gmail account using a web browser.

##Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure that you have:

- A computer or smartphone
- An internet connection
- A valid phone number for verification

##Procedure

Step 1: Open the Gmail Sign-Up Page

1. Open a web browser.
2. Navigate to the Gmail sign-up page.
3. Click Create Account.

Step 2: Enter Personal Information

1. Enter your first name and last name.
2. Choose a username.
3. Create a strong password.
4. Confirm the password.
5. Click Next.

Step 3: Verify Your Phone Number

1. Enter your phone number.
2. Click Next.
3. Enter the verification code sent to your phone.
4. Click Verify.

Step 4: Complete Account Setup

1. Enter your recovery email address (optional).
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Select your gender.
4. Click Next.

Step 5: Accept Terms and Conditions

1. Review the privacy and terms information.
2. Click I Agree.

##Result

Your Gmail account is successfully created and ready to use.

##Troubleshooting

Issue: Username Not Available

Cause: The selected username is already in use.

Solution: Choose a different username or add numbers to create a unique username.

Issue: Verification Code Not Received

Cause: Network delay or incorrect phone number.

Solution:

1. Verify the phone number.
2. Wait a few minutes.
3. Request a new verification code.

##Additional Information

For account security:

- Use a strong password.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Keep recovery information updated.
